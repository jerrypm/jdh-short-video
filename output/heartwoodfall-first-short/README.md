## Content and production

The video adapts Jerry PM's supplied Medium PDF, *Godot + Claude Opuse 5.5. Heartwoodfall part III — Combine Two Tools*, particularly pages 2, 5, and 6. It focuses on rebuilding the Three.js project in Godot while retaining the original as a design reference. Asset reuse is presented as a possibility that still needs testing.

Visuals combine the article's cover and Godot editor screenshot with original typography cards and gentle native motion. These are article screenshots, not recorded gameplay. The script was authored from the PDF; Gemini Nano was not used to generate it.

English narration was generated locally with Kokoro am_michael at 0.96 speed, then leveled locally. No cloud speech service or voice clone was used. The video has narration without background music.
